Good questions, thoughtful answers

Before we
begin.

Can we book décor without full wedding planning?+

Tell us which parts you want support with: décor, selected functions, guest coordination or the full celebration. The agreed proposal should make those responsibilities clear.

How do we work out a wedding budget?+

Start with your dates, venue, expected guests and functions. Add reference images and your priorities. These details make it possible to discuss scope and quotations; there is no single price that fits every celebration.

Can our Gujarati family use the checklist?+

Yes. Our checklist switches between Gujarati and English without losing your selections. It begins with Mandap Ropan and lets you include only the functions and services you need.

Can you work with vendors we have already chosen?+

List your booked vendors and their responsibilities in the enquiry or checklist. We can then discuss where additional support and coordination would be useful.

What should we prepare before a consultation?+

Your approximate dates, city or venue, number of guests, function list and budget range are a helpful start. A few photographs of styles you like are useful too.

What about outdoor functions and weather?+

Include a covered alternative, guest movement, power needs and the time required to change the setup in the venue discussion. The final arrangements depend on the location and agreed scope.
