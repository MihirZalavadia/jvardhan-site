For Venue Managers

The questions every property asks.

How do setup and dismantle windows work?

Timings are agreed in writing before the event — including access hours, load-in routes and the dismantle deadline. Protecting your next booking is part of our plan, not an afterthought.

How do you protect our property?

Non-invasive methods only: weighted bases instead of drilling or staking, protected flooring on load-in routes, and a walk-through with your team before and after every build.

Who coordinates with our team during an event?

One named supervisor, introduced to your team in advance, on-site and reachable for the full event. Your staff never has to chase a crowd of workers for answers.

What partnership models do you offer?

Preferred décor partner, approved vendor listing, co-branded wedding packages, or seasonal partnerships — and every relationship can begin with a low-risk three-event pilot before any long-term commitment.

What about insurance and liability?

Responsibility and coverage details are set out transparently in the written agreement for each partnership — we'd rather show you the paperwork in a meeting than make claims on a website.

What does a partnership cost the venue?

Commercial terms depend on the model you choose and are discussed openly after a site visit. No venue pays to be walked through, and the pilot is structured to be low-risk by design.
