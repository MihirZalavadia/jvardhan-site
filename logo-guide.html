The J.Vardhan Lion

J.VARDHANLUXE WEDDINGS & EVENTS

Theme, colours and full design system are being reworked separately — this brief is only about the lion. Mark drawn flat in gold #CFAA6D; must sit on both warm ivory and deep green (every specimen below is shown on the split ground).

Priority Direction — The Lion Face
Master emblem — refine this

Floral-mane face: keep the idea, refine the drawing

  • The mane made of marigolds + mogra buds is our most ownable idea — the family are flower people. Keep it.
  • Redraw as clean vector: max 8 large flower shapes, no tiny petals that clog when small.
  • Face calm and dignified, ear visible (Asiatic lions have short manes — the floral ruff stays contained, never a big round African mane).
  • Simple almond eye, clean nose wedge, closed mouth — no whisker dots.

Face variants worth sketching

  • Same face with a plain short mane (no flowers) — quieter corporate version
  • Face inside a plain double-ring roundel for stamps/avatars
  • Line-art only version (no solid fills) for foil/embroidery

Standing Lions — poses to explore fresh

Standing four-square

All four feet planted, head high, side profile — steady guardian at a gateway. Solid-shape style like the seated icon.

Walking, head high

The V0 pose redrawn properly: bold monoline, Asiatic anatomy, strokes thick enough to embroider.

Standing beneath a toran

Small lion under a slender toran archway — we build wedding gateways; the lion guards ours.

  • Asiatic (Gir) anatomy always: short sparse mane, ears visible, one belly-fold line, lean body, tail tuft (leaf-shaped tuft welcome)
  • Calm and dignified — never roaring, never hunting
  • Flat colour, consistent stroke logic, survives one-colour print and 24px
  • No crowns, shields, laurels, heraldry
  • No gems, gradients, gold-foil shine, 3D
  • Not the Gujarat Tourism lion; run a trademark/reverse-image check before finalising
