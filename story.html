The House of J.Vardhan

It began with flowers.

Before the mandaps, before the lighting rigs and the run-sheets, this family's trade was flowers — garlands tied by hand, mornings that smelled of marigold and mogra.

The flowers taught us the discipline: fresh means on time, beautiful means finished, and a family's biggest day allows no second attempt. Over the years the craft grew — structures, stages, gateways, light — until the house could carry a celebration from the first bloom to the last switched-off bulb.

Today J.Vardhan is that whole house under one name: florals, mandaps, lighting and planning, delivered by our own hands — for families who want to simply celebrate, and for venues that want one partner they can trust with their property.

Detailed family history and milestones will be published after documentation — we write nothing we cannot show.
